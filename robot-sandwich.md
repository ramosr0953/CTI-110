# BLT Sandwich

## Assumptions
- Robot knows how to operate kitchen appliances and has access to the necessary kitchen appliances, cookware, and utensils to perform this task
- Robot has its desired amounts and quantities of ingredients already set to the side
- Robot does not have feelings

## Ingredients
- Two slices of bread
- Six slices of thick cut bacon
- Four thin slices of tomatoes
- One cup of shredded lettuce
- Mayonnaise
- Cheese

## Instruction
1. Place the six slices of bacon flat on a baking sheet, evenly spread.
    *Do not overlap the bacon, if necessary use a second baking sheet for any excess bacon.
2. Open the oven door and place the baking sheet into the oven and then close the oven door.
3. Turn the oven on and set the cooking temperature to 420 Degrees Fahrenheit, when the oven reaches the desired temperature, remove the bacon from the oven.
4. Remove the bacon from the baking sheets, and place on paper towels on a plate to allow excess bacon grease to drain, then set to the side.
5. While the bacon is baking, take your slices of bread, and place them in the toaster.
6. When the bread is finished toasting, remove the slices of toast and place on a different plate.
7. Grab and open your mayonnaise jar by twisting the lid counter clockwise.
8. Then use a spreading knife to spread mayonnaise on one side of each slice of toast.
9. Place your bacon on the mayonnaise side of your first slice of toast.
10. Place your cheese on top of your bacon.
11. Spread your lettuce evenly across your cheese and bacon
12. Place your slices of tomatoes in each corner of the sandwich allowing the tomatoes to overlap near the center of the sandwich.
13. Top your sandwich with the last slice of toast, mayonnaise side down.

## Done when:
- The sandwich is completed.
