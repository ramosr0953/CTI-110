# BLT Sandwich

## Assumptions
- Two slices of bread
- Five slices of thick cut bacon
- Two slices of tomatos
- Handful of shredded lettuce
- Mayonnaise
- Cheese (optional)

## Instruction
1. Start by pan frying the bacon until desired finish
2. While the bacon is frying, toast your bread until desired finish
3. When the bread is finished toasting, spread the mayonnaise on one side of each slice of toast
4. Place your bacon on the mayonnaise side of your first slice of bread
5. Place your cheese (optional)
6. Place your lettuce
7. Place your tomatoes
8. Top your sandwich with the last slice of bread, mayonnaise side down

## Done When:
- The sandwich is completed.
